# DoTT background direction review

The owner rejected the first backyard for being insufficiently Day of the Tentacle-like and requested a refresh against the actual game. After seeing the redrawn backyard, they approved it with “Awesome” and requested “Now do SMART, Nortel, Shopify, and Lightspeed.” The four-room changes below are now implemented. That request authorizes the redraw; it is not approval of the resulting four images or a deployment instruction.

## References inspected

Six images from [Double Fine's official screenshot gallery](https://www.doublefine.com/games/day-of-the-tentacle-remastered) were downloaded outside the checkout and visually inspected: `dott-remastered-000`, `001`, `003`, `012`, `014` and `016`. Images `000`, `003`, `001` and `012` were the four generation references recorded in the provenance; `014` and `016` informed the study only. They are reference material only; none is published in the game.

The useful distinction is the construction of the drawing: distorted wall/floor planes, tapered and leaning architecture, eccentric prop proportions, large coherent colour shapes, irregular contours and selective surface detail. Purple walls, thick outlines and warm lighting alone do not establish that style. The screenshots contain both broad painted areas and restrained texture; the target is neither realistic environment concept art nor generic vector clip art.

## Backyard replacement

`public/art/backyard-hotdogs-dott.png` replaces the initial naturalistic yard. Four official images served as style references; the rejected yard supplied content/layout only. The replacement has a tapering house, sagging uneven fence, twisted trunk and graphic foliage, angular clouds, distorted table and squat off-kilter grill. Original composition and props are retained rather than copying a DoTT room, character or story element.

The initial PNG is archived at `.impeccable/review/backyard-initial-rejected.png`, outside the public assets. The initial favorable finish review did not establish a style match: the owner's rejection supersedes it. The replacement received a fresh independent source/screenshot review against official screenshots 000, 003 and 012, returning **SHIP** with no blocking style mismatch. The reviewer noted angular faceting somewhat stronger than DoTT's rubbery curves, without requiring another redraw. The owner's subsequent “Awesome” approves this backyard replacement and makes it the continuity reference for the next four rooms.

The replacement's image path and alt text are active in `src/game/rooms/backyard.ts`; its exact prompt matches both `docs/backyard-image-prompt.txt` and the PNG's `impeccable:prompt` text metadata. The production build/typecheck and six focused backyard production-browser tests passed after this revision. The 135 unit tests and full 72-test production-browser suite passed immediately before the image/path/alt-only revision, not after it. Current desktop and phone captures show the replacement and were the evidence for the fresh review. No deployment is established by these checks.

## Implemented four-room redraw

All four backgrounds were redrawn with built-in OpenAI image generation as opaque 1536×1024 PNGs. The prior room was the composition/prop authority; official screenshots [003](https://assets.doublefine.com/images/screenshots/DOTT/dott-remastered-003.jpg) and [012](https://assets.doublefine.com/images/screenshots/DOTT/dott-remastered-012.jpg) supplied style only; the approved backyard supplied continuity. Exact prompts are recorded in `docs/dott-room-image-prompts.md`, `docs/art-provenance.json` and each PNG's `impeccable:prompt` metadata. The generated pixels were copied unchanged. Previous room assets remain preserved.
